Confluence Olympia 1.0.0 - 2026-11-10

Initial release for Kodi 21 Omega, based on Confluence 5.0.9.
- Compact, fixed-position Fanart movie information panel.
- Original title, first director/writer, up to three genres, IMDb votes and RT AllCritics.
- Playback clearart and movie information keyart, with poster fallbacks.
- Centered media flags, selected-poster sizing and MPAA footer artwork.
- Four-column, two-row artwork picker with large thumbnails and side preview.
- Six configurable library-node submenu positions for Movies and TV Shows.
- Configurable Movies source filter with mixed movie-set grouping.
- Separate add-on ID, settings, helper properties and generated playlist.
